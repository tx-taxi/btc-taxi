Nodes, channels, and the network between them

Explore public Lightning nodes and channels, routing policies, network capacity, geographic distribution, and historical statistics. Follow a node’s connections or inspect the opening and closing of a channel.

Part of tx.taxi

Use the same search to find a Lightning node or channel, switch explorers, or route a transaction to its blockchain. Bitcoin’s on-chain explorer lives at btc.tx.taxi.

A public network view

Lightning payments and private channels are not publicly visible. Capacity describes the bitcoin committed to a public channel; it does not reveal either side’s current spendable balance. Node locations describe announced network endpoints.

Statistics show the source snapshot’s date. Cached data remains available during interruptions.
