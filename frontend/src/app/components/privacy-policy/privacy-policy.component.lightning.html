Privacy Policy

Updated: October 3, 2026


Public Network Data

lightning.btc.tx.taxi displays public Lightning gossip and network statistics. Private channels, individual payments, and channel balances are not publicly visible. For independent verification, compare the public graph with your own Lightning node.


Request Data

Normal website and API requests can reveal standard web metadata to the operator, including your IP address, user agent, requested URL, and request time. Server logs may be retained for reliability, abuse prevention, and debugging.


Local Preferences

Display preferences such as theme, denomination, fiat currency, language, and timezone may be stored in your browser. lightning.btc.tx.taxi does not require an account for ordinary explorer use.


Searches and Public Channels

Public Lightning node keys, announced endpoints, channel policies, and Bitcoin funding transactions are public. A node or channel lookup can associate that search with your network metadata. Do not treat a public explorer as a privacy boundary.


External Links

This site links to tx.taxi contact channels, Bitcoin documentation, upstream source code, and license information. Those external services have their own privacy policies.
